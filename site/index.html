What is Constellation 3D?

Constellation 3D turns the links in your Obsidian vault into an interactive, animated 3D map. Notes appear as stars and their links as connecting paths, so you can explore how ideas relate and find parts of your vault you may have forgotten. Your notes stay on your device: the plugin reads local note and link information and does not edit or upload your notes.

Explore your note network

View your whole vault, the notes around the current note, or its direct neighbors. Search notes and filter by folder, tag, date, or connection count.

Move through 3D

Rotate and zoom the space, open notes from their nodes, and follow highlighted connections between related notes.

21 visual styles

Choose Constellation, Timeline Map, Mind Palace, Circuit Minimal, Archive Fog, Focus Lens, Thread Weaver, Research Board, Signal Radar, Matrix Hacker, Star Map, Aqua Mint, Deep Space, Neon, Minimal, Soft Glow, Neural Bloom, Satellite View, Glass Minimal, Academic Light, or Ink Map.

Work with folder clusters

Group notes by top-level folder or full path. Right-click a note to collapse or expand its cluster, isolate that cluster, or hide it. Click a collapsed cluster summary to expand it again.

Stay responsive in large vaults

Search waits until you pause typing before rebuilding the graph. Large graphs automatically use lighter rendering; in very large graphs, line animations continue on a representative sample of the 12,000 strongest links.

Customize the 3D scene

Choose Nebula, Aurora, Star Map Grid, or Deep Void backgrounds; set particle count and color palette; and tune perspective depth, depth rings, node initials, and cluster halos.

Animate links and routes

Choose flowing particles, pulses, drawing lines, or moving dashes for connections, and select a separate animation for route previews.

Quick controls

Pause or resume animation from the graph, or open the in-view Control Panel to adjust graph, visual, motion, journey, and display settings without leaving the note space.

Move around the canvas

Drag a node to reposition it, drag the background to orbit, or switch to Pan Mode to move the whole view. Scroll to zoom.

Discover notes

Browse recent or forgotten notes, hubs, hidden gems, and orphans. Let the camera travel automatically or step through one note at a time.

Pin, hide, and preview routes

Keep important nodes in place, hide distracting ones, and preview a route through linked notes.

Make it yours

Pick from 26 independent color schemes, including Rainbow Flow, Heatmap, Galaxy Core, Tag Based, Folder Based, and custom palettes. Adjust labels, node initials, depth rings, cluster halos, node size, link thickness, and the FPS display.

Customize the interface

Toggle the header, graph title, node and link totals, Quick Menu, bottom dashboard, individual counters, labels, links, icons, depth guides, and cluster halos.

Download the plugin

Click Open latest release at the top of this page. Under Assets, download main.js, manifest.json, and styles.css. Save all three files somewhere easy to find, such as your Downloads folder.

Open your vault's plugins folder

Open the folder where your Obsidian vault is stored. Inside it, open .obsidian, then plugins. If .obsidian or plugins is missing, open Obsidian for this vault once and check again. If plugins is still missing, create a folder named exactly plugins inside .obsidian.

On a Mac

In Finder, open your vault folder and press Command + Shift + . to show hidden folders.

On Windows

In File Explorer, open your vault folder. Select View → Show → Hidden items.

Install the plugin folder

Inside .obsidian/plugins, create a folder named exactly constellation-three-d. Move the three downloaded files directly into it. Do not make another folder around them.

Your final folder should look exactly like this:

Your vault/.obsidian/plugins/constellation-three-d/main.js
Your vault/.obsidian/plugins/constellation-three-d/manifest.json
Your vault/.obsidian/plugins/constellation-three-d/styles.css

Check this carefully: manifest.json must be directly inside constellation-three-d, alongside the other two files.

Turn it on in Obsidian

Restart Obsidian. Open Settings → Community plugins. If Restricted mode is on, turn it off and confirm. Under Installed plugins, find Constellation 3D and switch it on.

Open your 3D note space

Press Command/Ctrl + P to open the command palette, search for Open Constellation 3D, and select it. You can also use the orbit icon in the left ribbon.

Something went wrong?

Constellation 3D is not listed? Confirm the path ends in .obsidian/plugins/constellation-three-d/manifest.json (with only one constellation-three-d folder), then restart Obsidian.

The graph is empty? Add links between notes using Obsidian's [[double brackets]], then click Refresh in Constellation 3D.

Still stuck? Open an issue on GitHub and include your operating system and Obsidian version.

Constellation 3D is a community plugin. Your notes stay in your vault; the plugin does not send them to a server.
